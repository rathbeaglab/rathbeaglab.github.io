# Binomial support

Binomial helps you learn to name plants from photos, in English and Latin (genus, species and cultivar or Group), for a horticulture plant identification test. It works offline and keeps your progress on your phone.

## Getting started

1. Tap **Study**. New plants come in small batches: look at the photos, guess, then see the answer.
2. Match the batch's photos to their names.
3. Come back each day. Plants you know well come back less often; ones you miss come back sooner.

Every screen has small **?** buttons that explain what you're looking at. Settings → **How it works** lists them all.

## Common questions

**Where is my progress stored?** Only on your phone. To move it to a new phone, use Settings → **Export backup**, then **Import backup** on the new phone.

**Can I add my own photos?** Yes. Use **Name a plant** on the home screen, or the gallery on any plant's page. Your photos are shown first in later reviews.

**A photo looks wrong.** Open the plant's page, find the photo in the gallery and hide it. Please also tell us, so it can be replaced for everyone.

**Which version do I have?** Settings → **About this app**.
