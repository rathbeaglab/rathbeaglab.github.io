# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Binomial is a study app for learning to identify plants and their Latin names. It is built to keep everything on your phone.

## What the app collects

**Nothing about you.** Binomial has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your progress:** which plants you have learned, your answers and your review schedule. These are kept in a database on your device.
- **Your photos:** photos you take or choose in the app (to practise naming plants you find) are copied into the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere.
- **Your settings:** theme, daily reminder time and similar choices.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## Camera and photo library

The app asks for the camera and photo library only when you choose to add a photo of a plant. You can refuse, and the rest of the app works as normal. Photos are only used inside the app.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your phone. It is not sent from a server, and no notification token is collected.

## Backups

You can export a backup file and import it again. The file goes only where you send it through your phone's share sheet (for example, your own Files or cloud storage). We never receive it.

## App updates

When it opens, the app checks for an updated version through Expo's update service (EAS Update, run by 650 Industries, Inc.). This request contains technical details needed to deliver the right update: the app version, the platform (iOS or Android), the update channel, and a random installation identifier that is not linked to you. Your IP address is visible to the service, as with any internet request. None of your progress, photos or settings is sent. See [Expo's privacy policy](https://expo.dev/privacy).

## Plant photos

The plant photos bundled with the app come from [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org) under Creative Commons licences. Each photo's author and licence are listed in the app under Settings → Photo credits.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
